How it works

When you upload a file, ANTVRS computes its SHA-256 fingerprint and checks it against a list of known-malicious files. It also runs detection rules and, where available, the ClamAV engine.

Limitations

  • No scanner detects all malware. New or custom malware often goes undetected.
  • A "no threats detected" result does not prove a file is safe.
  • Detection rules can produce false positives on legitimate files.
  • We do not run or open your file, so we cannot observe what it does.
  • The engines listed on each result page show exactly what was checked.
